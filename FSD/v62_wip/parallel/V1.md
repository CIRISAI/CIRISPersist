## Mechanism (`edition2::voices`, read-checked against T1's vouch)

- **`ingest::put_admitted_row`**: its tail (the "further voice" branch plus the put_attestation path) is replaced by `voices::put_counted_row(dir, admitted, tier)`.
  - It runs after T1's group branch, O1's occurrence branch and (once merged) T2's typed branch, and after the key plane.
  - The `SignedAttestation` import was dropped from ingest.rs because it is now unused.
- **`put_counted_row`**:
  - If the typed row is held, the voice is kept and the answer is `Voice`.
  - Otherwise the row is projected from its **primary voice**: the author's own voice for an authored row, else the first held voice that still re-admits under the current key plane, else the offered voice.
  - It then runs `put_attestation` inside a task-local `ATTEMPT` scope.
    - While the scope is active, `voices_of_row` counts the OFFERED voice alongside the held ones. So every `counted_roster_signers` / `row_voice_issuers` reader counts the voice now arriving before it is kept.
  - Outcomes:
    - **Ok**: the primary and the offered voice are kept, and the answer is `Row`.
    - **Err with `note_short()` set** (structural, never matched on the error text): the offered voice is kept and the answer is `Pending`. No typed row is written.
    - **Any other Err**: refused, and nothing is kept.
- **`note_short()`** is a no-op outside the attempt, so the earlier form and read-time folds are unchanged.
