## What changed, file by file

### `src/federation/edition2/payloads.rs` (owned)

- **`Ty`:**
  - NEW variant `Ty::Any` (`ceg-value`), used for takedown's `perceptual_hash`, whose type the text leaves OPEN.
  - `Ty::Digests` now means the envelope's `[+ hash-digest]`: a non-empty SEQUENCE in signed order, no longer a sorted set.
  - `Ty::RosterMembers` is now a set by each element's encoding (`sorted_unique`), plus one entry per `key`, plus each entry checked against `ROSTER_MEMBER` with no extra members.
- **`Presence::RequiredWhen`** is now `(&'static str, &'static [&'static str])`, meaning "any of". Nothing outside this file names `Presence` (read-checked across all `CIRISPersist-wp-*` trees).
- **FAMILIES:**
  - **group:roster:**
    - `refs.prev` becomes `refs.head`, and `refs.also:charter` becomes `refs.also:cites`;
    - new members: `cohort_subkind` (tstr, O), `cohort_subkind_payload` (map, O) and `membership` (tstr, O; closed `private|public`);
    - `consensus_protocol_entrenched` is a bool (O, absent reads false).
  - **membership:{stage}:** `refs.head` is REQUIRED; new `refs.also:cites` (O); `reason` is Uint; new `valid_until` (Ms, O).
  - **identity:occurrence_revocation:** `reason` is Uint.
  - **cosign:{state}:**
    - no members and no refs;
    - the `lineage_head` state is removed (the leaves are closed: `pending|signature`);
    - the payload is the event's own (`OVER_THE_EVENT`), so its members are not checked here.
  - **revocation:** the family `reason` member is removed. `reason` is now the common envelope uint.
  - **takedown_notice:**
    - `legal_basis` is closed to `LEGAL_BASES` (11) and must equal the leaf;
    - new `perceptual_hash` (Any, O) and `counter_notice_channel` (tstr, O);
    - the authorship text adds the affiliations case;
    - a steward purge carries no `author`.
  - **custody:** `expires_at` and `cohort` are removed; `custody_state` is closed `here|none`; `evidence_refs` holds exactly one digest.
    - A custody payload that CARRIES `expires_at` is refused `edition2_payload_invalid`: the common envelope member is excluded for this family by a prose rule, so the map stays closed.
  - **key_grant:**
    - `owner` is required on content and stream, and `epoch` on epoch and stream;
    - `wrap_algorithm` is closed to `at_rest_cascade::WRAP_ALGORITHM_V2`;
    - `community_id` and `cohort` are removed (they are the CC 2.1 common members).
  - **delivery_receipt:** the voice text now says there is no inner signed object, and the earlier preimage is CC 8.11.30. The `k` member is unchanged.
- **New public items:**
  - `LEGAL_BASES`, `HASH_DIGEST_MEMBERS` (`evidence_refs`, `carried`, `seat_commitment`, `amendment_digest`);
  - `ALSO_ROLES`, `enum Leaves { Closed, Mapped }`, `LEAVES`, `CLOSED_VALUES`, `OVER_THE_EVENT`, `PROSE_RULES`, `BODY_NOTE_MAX`.
- **`COMMON`** is widened to the CDDL `attestation-envelope`'s named members plus the CC 2.1 members `cohort_scope`, `community_id`, `family_id`, `subject_key_ids` and `delivery_mode`.
  - Custody is the exception: its map refuses `expires_at` (the prose rule above).
  - New private `COMMON_TYPED` types `asserted_at` (required), `author`, `dimension`, `refs`, `reason` (uint), `body` (map), `evidence_refs`, `seat_commitment`, `valid_from`, `valid_until`, `expires_at` and `salt`.
- **`check()`**, in order:
  1. leaf rules;
  2. `names_of`;
  3. the `also` roles closed (every mapped family);
  4. return early for `OVER_THE_EVENT`;
  5. common types and the `body.note` bound (≤2048 bytes);
  6. the member list;
  7. `CLOSED_VALUES`;
  8. the required refs;
  9. the prose rules: roster head iff version>1, the takedown leaf equals `legal_basis`, a steward purge has no author, custody has exactly one evidence digest.

  A membership stage other than widening/removal returns `Ok` (its forward payload is OPEN). Every refusal is still `edition2_payload_invalid`.
- **`render_markdown()`** also renders the leaves, the payload-over-the-event line, the rules section per family, and a closing "Every mapped family" section.

## Known compile risks

1. **`Ty::Any` breaks every exhaustive `match` on `Ty` outside this file.** E0's `fixture_switch.rs::typed_value` matches Ty exhaustively, read-checked in `/home/emoore/CIRISPersist-wp-E0`. Its fix is to add `| Ty::Any` to the `cbor_of` arm; I messaged E0. On syn0 no other match exists.
2. **Pattern and coercion typing in the new code**:
   - match ergonomics through `&&(&str, Leaves)` in `LEAVES.iter().find(|(fam, _)| ...)`, and `(leaves, leaf_of(..))` with `Leaves::Closed(set)` against `&Leaves`;
   - the unsized coercions of `&["a","b"]` inside the `CLOSED_VALUES` / `LEAVES` const tuples;
   - the `fail` closure is reused across several `map_err(fail)` calls, which relies on it being `Copy` (it captures only `&str`).
3. **The I2102 doc-render equality** (see above), and the `#[track_caller]` helpers in tests. These are low risk.
