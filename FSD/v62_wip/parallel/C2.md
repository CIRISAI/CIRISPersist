- I2340 (CC 4.2.6: a spent recovery commitment never returns). The pure test is `i2340_a_spent_recovery_commitment_never_returns`; the store test is `store::i2340_i2341_{sqlite,postgres}`, through C1's door. Over a recovered lineage, three bundles must each conclude nothing (`genesis_bundle_count_unmet` / `recovery_commitment_spent`): a second recovery under the spent recovery key, a second recovery that commits the spent key again (pure only), and a roster bundle that re-commits it. On the store, the door refuses each and the head stays the recovery. The controls are Valid / installed: a second recovery under the committed key, and a re-commit to a fresh key (pure).
- I2341 (CC 4.2.6: a recovery key is refused as a signing key, by its public key material). The pure test is `i2341_a_recovery_key_is_refused_as_a_signing_key`, plus the same store tests. A recovery that seats the recovery key itself as the signing key is `genesis_bundle_malformed` / `recovery_key_is_signing_key`. The pure test does this on the genesis, the store test on holder 2 over the recovered lineage. A recovery onto a fresh key is the control. On the store, after the recovery names the spent recovery key's record, a `key:revocation` row is offered to `put_statement`, signed by that key's material under the holder's identity. It must be a FINAL refusal: `Err`, and the error is neither `Error::AttesterKeyUnknown` (the retryable unknown-signer wait, which is also the edition-2 door's Waiting) nor `Error::Backend`. No voice may be held (`edition2_voices`) and no row (`get_attestation`). The exact token is unpinned.
  **LIKELY DOOR GAP:** persist's key plane looks up a statement's signer through `edition2_statements_about(key:record, identity_ref(kid))`. A recovery key's record has `sub` = the HOLDER's identity, not the key's own, so the door will probably answer `AttesterKeyUnknown` (a wait), and the witness goes red. The fix at synthesis belongs in the door, not the witness: a signer whose key material is a committed or spent recovery key of the lineage in force (`GenesisPlacement::{lineage_commitments, spent_commitments}`, matched by `recovery_commitment` over the key) must be refused finally. If the door instead ADMITS the row (`Ok`), that is the worse gap and the same fix applies.
