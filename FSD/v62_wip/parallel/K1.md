### key_end through the kernel (CC 2.4.1.1)

- **New `src/kernel/key_end.rs`**
  - `KeyEndView` implements `Row` and `KeyEndRow` (T = u64 ms).
  - `end_rule(identity_type)`: node/agent → `Required`, user/accord_holder → `Forbidden`, otherwise

    of kids whose voice over the row verified.
- **`src/federation/edition2/liveness.rs`**

    comes from `kernel::key_end::key_live` over `key_end_views(..)` (the records, plus the key's OWN
    honoured revocations).
  - The `Live` record reported is the covering one with the greatest end, among the kernel's kept
    rows.
  - `ValidRecord` gains `pub signer: Digest32`; the only constructor is in `judged` (*read-checked*).
  - `revoked()` is folded into `in_effect()`.
  - The TODO(key_end) sites are rewritten.
- **The boundary is ONE function** (RULED half-open everywhere):
  `crate::kernel::key_end::within_record(at: u64, from: u64, until: Option<u64>) -> bool`, which is
  `from <= at && until.is_none_or(|u| at < u)`. It is published in `K1.api.md` and O1 calls it.
  - `liveness.rs`'s `covers()` now delegates to it. So does every coverage reading in the file: a
    key's liveness filter, the key-of-issuer chain's predecessor record covering a rotation's instant
    (step 4 of `key_of_at_depth`), and the rival-rotation test. These are cut A's chain checks. They
    live in `liveness.rs`, so there was no other file to hunk.
  - A test-only `inclusive()` keeps the former reading as the differential's reference.
  - The differential class `key_end_half_open` (`Class::KeyEndHalfOpen`, `differential::key_end`) names
    the one instant where the readings differ.
  - Verify 23.1.3's `KeyRecordDirectory` still reads inclusive (`at > u` → Invalid; *read-checked*).
    Verify follows in 23.3.1.
  - `halt.rs`'s replay window was already half-open (`asserted_at < u`; *read-checked*), so it is
    untouched.
- **Third-party revokers are KEPT** (RULED): `RevokerStanding`, owner and roster, sits on top of the
  kernel answer.
  - **Kernel friction for CC and CIRISKernel:** `key_end::live` / `ended` / `retained` count only the
    key's own revocation (`revoked_from` filters `signer == key`). Eric's ruling is that three parties
    may end a key (the key itself, its owner, and the roster that seats it), and CC 2.4.1.1's sentence
    "No other row enters that reading, so no third party ends a key" contradicts it.
  - The kernel needs either a revoker-standing input or a cell for owner and roster revocations. Until
    then, persist reads `Revoked` before asking the kernel.

## 6. NOT done, and what K2 / synthesis needs

1. **Roster as "the members common to all tips"** (the second half of CC 2.7.8) is not wired. **Ruled: left to T1 (typed group projections).** Tips are
   held, listed and inert. The head's roster still decides every quorum, which is no weaker than the
   former refusal. The sites that would read it:
   - `FederationDirectory::verify_membership_quorum` (`mod.rs`, the roster built from
     `active_*_members`);
   - the backends' `active_family_members` / `active_community_members`;
   - `roster_head` folds.
   Each tip is quorum-verified against the head at admission, so it is safe to count. Careful: a tip's
   record `members` is the base record, and widenings ride the planes.
2. **"A version naming the tips makes one head again"** is not wired. `group:roster` names a single
   `prev` (`payloads.rs`), so no record can name two tips. The edition-2 refs grammar has
   `further_heads` (`ceg_row_names` role list, V300). A merge needs the CC payload rule, then
   `prev_head_names_held` / `head_entries` reading several names. Tips are never pruned except by
   becoming the head.
3. **No automatic promotion of a waiting tip** when its named version becomes the head. It extends
   only when re-offered (replication re-offers). The executor could promote it, but that needs the
   quorum re-run, which is async outside the tx.
4. **Delegation cycle gate and advisory lock: NOT removed**, because other code needs the blocks:
   - the test race points `pause_if_armed("delegation_cycle_before_insert" / "…_before_crossing")`
     live inside the lock blocks and are armed by `serve_feeds_1163_1190_invariants.rs:1310`;
   - `delegation_cycle_invariants` is the fixture provider for `reseal_race_invariants`,
     `genesis_purge_door_invariants` and `one_connection_pool_invariants`.

   Sites, if K2 / synthesis removes them (the gate refuses nothing since pass 1):
   - `admission.rs`: `check_delegation_cycle_admission` (~18854), `delegation_cycle_gated` (~18922),
     `DELEGATION_CYCLE_LOCK_KEY` (~18942), `delegation_path_hops` (~18968), and the call at ~3433
     (promotion);
   - `attestation_admit.rs`: `cycle_gated` field and method (172–194), `recheck_cycle_under_lock`, and
     the calls at 1172 / 1389 / 1415;
   - `postgres.rs`: `delegation_write_lock`, `PgTxDelegationGraph`, the crossing block ~14557–14610,
     and the insert block ~21950–22016;
   - `sqlite.rs`: ~187, ~7790–7806 and ~16118–16123;
   - `parity.rs`: the `check_delegation_cycle_admission` row and the expected lists ~1538 / ~3460 /
     ~3496;
   - `kernel/differential.rs`: `cycle_gate` and `Class::CycleVisitedOnce`.
5. **The genesis-record owner path has no producer** (see §1). It waits on CC's genesis payload rule.
6. **`evidence/migration_checksums.tsv` V300 rows** need re-pinning (refinery checksum; needs a build).
