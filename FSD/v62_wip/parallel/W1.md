## (1) The trust-root liveness margin, re-pointed to the bundle in force

**Gap.** The trust-root door counted a trust-root community's birth against the accord FAMILY record `humanity-accord`, and seated holder-founders by that family's roster. A v62 node never holds that record, so no trust-root-grade community could land, and T7's stalled/restored legs were unreachable.

**Change** (`canonical_community.rs`, helpers in `trust_root_group.rs`):

| read | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| holder arm (`accord_holder_seated_at`, `check_founder_eligible`, `Memo::founder_counts`) | the accord family's folded roster | first: a holder identity-ref of the bundle in force, common to every tip; the earlier read after. `founder_counts` / `check_founder_eligible` no longer need a `federation_keys` row for a bundle holder. |
| birth count (`accord_quorum_at`) | hybrid scrubs vs the family | when the version has edition-2 voices: distinct bundle holders among the voices ≥ ⌊n/2⌋+1 of the bundle's holders (CC 3.2 "a co-signature by a strict majority of the standing accord roster", citing CC 2.7.5; CC 2.6.1.11). Earlier-form rows keep the family path. |
| a founders' link (`verify_founders_link_memo`) | `ThresholdSignature`s over the change envelope + the authority's hybrid scrub | edition-2 versions: their voices (held statements + the door's vouch) counted as prior founders that count at `amended_at`; the single-authority check and `verify_community_admission` are skipped for them (the count is the authorization, and the markers verify only under the vouch) |
| a short count at the door | refused | `note_short()` in `insufficient()` and the link's shortfall, so T1 keeps the voice (`Pending`) instead of refusing it |
| standing cache key | — | adds the bundle's holder refs and the community's held roster voices |

"The version's voices" (`version_signers`) = the `iss` of every held `group:roster` statement whose re-derived typed record hashes to the version's `persist_row_hash` (T1's `held_head` method), ∪ the vouched voices when the vouch names the record's signing envelope (the last voice is not stored until after the door). `None` → the earlier reading.

T1's group door: a trust-root version reaching `put_community` carries `lineage = chain_of(held)` (the payload carries none), so `check_trust_root_community_admission` sees the chain EXTEND the held version and walks the link. The change envelope is bound by `bind_next_version` (founder roles, `next_persist_row_hash`, `amended_at` = the row's `asserted_at`), as the link check requires. Routing: an occupied trust-root id goes `OccupiedCommunity::TrustRootChain`, so `apply_trust_root_chain_counted` (read-checked), not the generic supersede and not its cannot-add rule.

## (2) A counted `transport:canonical_rebind`

CC holds it (read-checked: `part_3` l.147, 3.3.6.2 "A seat's transit key", 3.4.7 row, `CLM-canonical-rebind`). Verify's family model says `"counted"` (`data/cc/family_authorship.json`). Verify does not enforce the reserved signer, so persist does.

- **Payload** (P1's table): `transit_xwing_pubkey` bstr R (1216 checked at the door, `xwing_pubkey_wrong_length`), `onion_hop_xwing_pubkey` bstr O, `transport_destination` map O, `refs.head` R (the bundle in force, by object id). Counted (no `author`), `sub` = the seat, row type `scores`.
- **Door** (`put_rebind_voice`, reached from `put_admitted_row`):
  - no genesis held → waits (retryable unknown signer);
  - `refs.head` absent → `edition2_payload_invalid`; names an unheld bundle → waits; names a held bundle not in force → `edition2_payload_invalid`;
  - `sub` not a seat of the bundle in force, or the signer not a holder of it at its placed kid → `reserved_family_unseated`;
  - else kept in `ceg_statements`, `Row` once the distinct holder voices reach ⌊n/2⌋+1, `Pending` before. No `federation_attestations` row: the earlier `check_rebind_admission` (JSON `SignedTransportDestination`, `lookup_family`) never sees it.
- **Read** (`seat_transit_key`, from `transit_xwing_pubkey_for`, which is the point where the rule is enforced): for a seat of the bundle in force, the key is the latest (`asserted_at`, then row id) re-bind whose `refs.head` names a bundle of the run that seeded the seat's current binding, counted against THAT bundle's holders; else the bundle binding's key. The seat's own route-plane bindings are never read for it. A later bundle that re-seats the seat with a new key resets it.

**The count (CC ruling):** a strict majority of the holders of the bundle in force, over the `refs.head` naming that bundle (`transport_rebind::required_count`). A later bundle re-seating the node on new X-Wing keys is the other path; both stand.

Secondary, not built: the cross-row X-Wing rule (CC 3.3.6.2: a half equal to another key of the node) is not applied to a re-bound key. The earlier resolver's check is bypassed for seats of the bundle in force.
