## P2c — held, not refused (CC 2.7)

Ruling: CC 2.7, "a row is never refused for what another row says". The identity_amendment gate reads a second row (the approving deferral response), so it must not refuse at the door.

### The door (`check_identity_amendment_admission`) now refuses ONLY what the amendment itself decides

- the member shapes (`MalformedRow`, unchanged);
- `evidence_refs[]` not citing `authorized_by` (`identity_amendment_unauthorized` / `evidence_does_not_cite_response`), which is the row's own bytes;
- the signer not being an active occurrence of the agent (`identity_amendment_unauthorized` / `attester_not_the_agents_occurrence`). This asks about the SIGNER, not the cited response, and I kept it as your list implied. **Lead, decide:** it reads the occurrence rows, so if CC 2.7 is meant to cover the signer's standing too, it becomes a sixth held condition. That is a five-line move.

The door no longer reads the response at all.

### The standing, read at the time of the read

`identity_amendment::amendment_standing(dir, &row) -> Result<Option<AmendmentStanding>, Error>`:
- It returns `None` for a row that is not an amendment.
- `AmendmentStanding::{InForce, Held { token, reason, detail }}` has the methods `is_in_force()`, `token()` and `reason()`.
- The conditions, read in order over the cited response as held now:
  1. not held: `identity_amendment_unauthorized` / `response_not_held`;
  2. not approved: `…/response_not_approved`;
  3. digest: `amendment_digest_mismatch` / its three cases;
  4. about another agent: `…/response_about_another_agent`;
  5. signer not seated: `…/response_signer_not_a_seated_wa`.
- Nothing is stored. A later read sees a later approval, so an amendment that arrives ahead of its approval counts once the approval lands.

`identity_amendment::amendment_standings_for(dir, agent) -> Result<Vec<(Attestation, AmendmentStanding)>, Error>` returns every `identity_amendment:*` row about `agent`, each with its standing. Retraction is the reader's usual fold and is not applied here.

**Readers:** persist has no existing reader of "the amendment in force" (grepped: no code reads `identity_amendment:` rows except the gate). These two functions ARE the read. Server/consumers must ask them, not the bare row's presence. There is no FFI/pyo3 surface yet (not asked; say if Server needs one).

**Following the held patterns:**
- T1 and V1 hold a *voice* short of a protocol: the door keeps it and answers `Pending`.
- An amendment has no voice shortfall. The row itself is complete, and only its force depends on another row. So it lands as an ordinary `Inserted` row, and its force is a read-time fold, the same shape as `licensure::status_set_for` ("testimony stays stored; the fold decides").
- No `Pending` outcome or new table is needed.

**Not followed (needs a ruling):** an approval that is *superseded* by its WA (`supersedes` with a corrected `amendment_digest`) is NOT followed. The standing reads the cited row id as held. A digest mismatch is therefore permanent for that amendment row unless the WA's supersession is resolved. Say if the standing should resolve the live version of the cited response.

### P2c rulings (lead) and the withdrawal condition

1. **The signer-occurrence check stays a door refusal.** CC lists "an occurrence row of iss" as part of the signer's key plane, which the door may read. No change.
2. **`supersedes` is NOT followed.** The amendment names a specific approval row. A later or different approval is answered by re-emitting the amendment citing it (Server's path).
3. **A WITHDRAWAL is followed.** This is the new held condition (2a), with the new reason `unauthorized_reason::RESPONSE_WITHDRAWN = "response_withdrawn"` under the token `identity_amendment_unauthorized`.
   - Read in `amendment_standing` right after "approved".

   - Never a door refusal.
   - **I2935** (sqlite + pg, in `amendment_digest_invariants`): an amendment in force; the WA (`owner`, the approval's producer) withdraws its approval via `ts::put_retraction`; the same amendment now reads held (`response_withdrawn`) and stays stored.    - Mutation targets: the `retracts &&` test and the `retraction_entitled` call.
   - Compile/run risk: I2935's `withdraws` by the owner relies on the withdraws door admitting a producer's retraction of its own `self`-scope row, as I1363's `put_retraction` does for a subject.
