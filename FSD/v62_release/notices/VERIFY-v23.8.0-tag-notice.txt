A GENESIS BUNDLE MAY JOIN TIPS (edition2::genesis; CC 2.6.1.11)
- refs.further_prev: a set of at least one object reference, beside prev, never naming prev again
  (genesis_bundle_schema; unsorted: federation_set_semantics_unsorted).
- version = 1 + the greatest version among the predecessors named; prev = the one of greatest version, least
  object id (version_prev_mismatch); one family_key_id; a predecessor in another's lineage past is Invalid
  further_prev_not_newest (Malformed::FurtherPrevNotNewest).
- Waiting names every predecessor not held (GenesisWaiting.further_prev beside .prev); one held and not Valid is
  genesis_bundle_prev_not_valid.
- The count: a strict majority of EACH named predecessor's live holders, each voice under the key that
  predecessor seats. A holder one branch rotated signs under each of its keys. A signing key a counted RECOVERY
  replaced is no voice on any branch (its statement refused alone, statement_kid_not_iss) and the holder a
  predecessor still seats at it is out of that predecessor's live holders. A predecessor with no live holder:
  genesis_bundle_count_unmet. A holder no predecessor seats signs; so does a holder whose recovery commitment
  differs from the one any named predecessor carries.
- A join inherits the union: seating a key any branch retired or left is Invalid holder_key_retired; naming a
  commitment any branch spent is recovery_commitment_spent.
- A recovery carries no further_prev (recovery_changes_other_seat).
- A recovery whose prev is in the past of a held Valid bundle its holder signed, and is none of the predecessors
  that bundle names, concludes nothing: Inconclusive::RecoveryBehindHead, token recovery_behind_head, no detail.
  A sibling on the same prev is a fork, and both are read on their own.
- GenesisInput.predecessors is now every bundle of the lineage the reader holds, not only the one prev names.
- GenesisPlacement gains further_prev, past, recovery_retired_keys, voices (each verified statement as iss, kid),
  predecessors() and regime() (the charter's deterministic encoding without recovery_commitments).

WHAT A READER ACTS ON AT SEVERAL TIPS (CC 3.2 T6, CC 2.7.8)
- New pure module edition2::root_fold (Lineage, RootBundle, Reader, ReaderAnchor, InForce, KeyRevocation,
  GENESIS_JOIN_CONTESTED): a port of CIRISKernel models/trust_root/root.py (root-fold 272fcd6). Tips of one regime
  fold; tips whose regimes differ give what they share; two joins of the same predecessors under different
  regimes are contested (a state; neither stands; the reader keeps the tips they tried to join); two recoveries of
  one seat likewise; a self-forked bundle is void where the other side outnumbers it; in force is per reader.
- genesis::in_force(valid: &[&GenesisPlacement], anchor: &Anchor, acknowledged: &[Digest32]) -> Option<ActOn>
  ActOn { heads, holders: Vec<PlacedHolder>, seats: Vec<PlacedSeat>, folded, contested }.
  `acknowledged`: the bundles the reader's owner acknowledged, in order (the attached_head_digest of its
  trust:accepts root edges). None acknowledged: the anchor's first bundle. Under a thumbprint anchor, two first
  bundles that match root on neither (None).
- Holder-key revocations are NOT read yet: in_force takes every seated holder as live.
