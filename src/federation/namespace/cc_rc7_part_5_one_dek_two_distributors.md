**Rekey conforms to MLS TreeKEM ([RFC 9420](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9420), normative).** The epoch-DEK rekey on member change is the MLS TreeKEM construction: an O(log N) path rekey, the commit signed once, with RFC 9420 blank-node / unmerged-leaf handling and parent-hash tree integrity. The hybrid KEM (X25519+ML-KEM-768) is the MLS ciphersuite's HPKE KEM. **One DEK per `(stream_id, epoch)`, two distributors (normative — [CIRISConstitution#140](https://github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISConstitution/issues/140)).** For the live participants of a call the group's MLS **exporter secret for that epoch is the epoch DEK** — MLS is the rekey and live distributor, and there is no second key. The same DEK is wrapped as `key_grant:stream:v1` rows ([CC 3.3.2](part_3_the_namespace.md), one per recipient, `wrap_algorithm: v2`) for late joiners, recordings and anyone the audience admits — **only for a stream that is kept**: one that is recorded, or whose `history_on_join` is `full`. An unrecorded live call wraps nothing: its DEK exists only inside the live group, so an ordinary call keeps the forward secrecy MLS gives it and leaves no durable wrap that could later open it. (rc6 had edge's live DEK come from MLS and never be wrapped, and persist's stored DEK minted by persist and never reach MLS; that fork is closed.)
